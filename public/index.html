04 About

I'm a software engineer at Amazon, working on a data pipeline platform that serves petabytes of data every day. My work spans the control plane — the APIs, orchestration, and scheduling that run pipeline operations — and the data plane — the cloud-native compute that executes them: Lambda for serverless ingest, EC2 fleets for processing, and EMR for large-scale transforms.

I design systems that make pipelines easier to operate and cheaper to run. Most of my work comes back to efficiency: improving compute and storage so customers get their data faster, at lower cost, at every scale.
